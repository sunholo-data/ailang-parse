Product X: User Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for Product X. This guide provides comprehensive information on installing, configuring, and utilizing all features of Product X to enhance your workflow. We aim to make your experience as smooth and productive as possible.

Installation Guide

  1. **Download the Installer:** Visit our official website and download the latest version of Product X for your operating system.
  2. **Run the Installer:** Execute the downloaded installer file. Follow the on-screen prompts, agreeing to the license terms and choosing your preferred installation directory.
  3. **Complete Setup:** Once the installation wizard finishes, click 'Finish'. Product X shortcuts will be added to your desktop and Start menu (or Applications folder on macOS).
  4. **Initial Launch & Activation:** Launch Product X. On the first run, you will be prompted to enter your license key. Enter the key provided at purchase to activate the software.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is Product X compatible with my operating system?** A: Product X supports Windows 10 (64-bit), macOS 10.15 Catalina or later, and most modern Linux distributions (Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian). Please refer to the system requirements section for detailed information.

**Q: How do I update Product X to the latest version?** A: Product X periodically checks for updates automatically. When an update is available, you will receive a notification within the application. You can also manually check for updates via 'Help' > 'Check for Updates' in the application menu.

**Q: Where can I find my license key?** A: Your license key was sent to your registered email address immediately after purchase. If you cannot find it, please check your spam folder or contact our support team with your purchase details.
